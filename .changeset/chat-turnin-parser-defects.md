Three counterparty turn-in shapes from live fills 2026-09-13 now parse, and the moneyline catch-all fails closed:

- **Fused bare prefix.** `YGNY Mets vs Miami Marlins O0.5 1st inning +108 = 5.0` threw UnrecognizedChatPrefixError("YGNY"). IW/YG glued to 2-3 UPPERCASE letters (a team abbreviation typed without the space) de-fuses to `YG NY …` and parses like the spaced message. The shape is narrow on purpose — `IWant`, `iwill`, `YGX`, `IWANTED` are still not prefixes — and `EXPLICIT_PREFIX_SIGNAL` recognizes the same shape (new export `FUSED_BARE_PREFIX`).
- **"A vs B" matchups.** `Cincinnati Reds vs LA Dodgers U0.5 1st inning -108` used to land as ONE participant named "Cincinnati Reds vs LA Dodgers". A whitespace-delimited `vs` / `vs.` (any case) now splits exactly like `/`: two-team totals get Team1/Team2, a spread keeps the slash form's contestant (Team1), and a matchup with no side or line throws InvalidContractTypeError like `Yankees/Red Sox` does. A name ending in "vs" (Cavs) never splits.
- **un/und and ov/ovr.** `yg h1 mil un 4 -120 = 3k` minted a moneyline on the contestant "mil un 4"; it is a half-1 match total, line 4, under, -120. The clippings are rewritten to the full words before any grammar rule reads the contract text (new export `OVER_UNDER_CLIPPINGS`), so glued prices (`un 4-105`, `ovr8.5-115`) and the implied-prefix gate (`BET_CANDIDATE_SIGNAL`) follow.
- **BREAKING (fail-closed moneylines).** A moneyline contestant must be a name: every token digit-free or an allowlisted digit-led name (`49ers`, `76ers`). Text that reaches the moneyline paths (plain name, `ML`, `+0/-0`) with any other digit — `mil un 4`, `Guardians-128`, `Yankees 7`, a matchup whose second team is `Pirates u8.5` — now throws InvalidContractTypeError instead of silently booking a moneyline on it. `YG Guardians-128 @ -115 = 2k` (explicit price beating a glued number) previously parsed as a moneyline on "Guardians-128"; it now throws, per the 0.8.0 intent that a contradictory glued number fails loudly. A moneyline marker left beside a total (`mil under 4 ML`, `Padres/Pirates u8.5 +0`) throws the same way.
